## 3. Answers to the 10 Human Review Questions (Section 52)

### 1. What percentage of the original 429 TRUE_CONFLICT classifications survived strict falsification?
> **0.0% (0 / 429 edges survived).**
> Under the six locked criteria of Section 4 (Material Scope Overlap, Compatible Preconditions, Actionable Incompatibility, Context Inability to Reconcile, Modality Non-Removal, and Source Meaning Contradiction), every single edge failed at least two conditions. Most prominently, 39.3% were separated by workflow lifecycle stages, 20.5% by modality collapse across different security actions, 19.5% by unrelated polysemous pairings, 10.0% by cross-platform boundaries, and 9.1% by framework generation disparities.

### 2. What was the dominant source of false conflicts?
> **`WORKFLOW_STAGE_COLLAPSE` (262 edges, 39.3%)**, closely followed by **`MODALITY_COLLAPSE` (137 edges, 20.5%)** and **`PAIRING_FALSE_POSITIVE` (130 edges, 19.5%)**.
> The candidate generator's heuristic that paired mandatory instructions with negative constraints caused disparate commands in multi-phase tools (like GSD) or defense-in-depth security packages (like Android Intent Security) to be falsely flagged as opposing imperatives.

### 3. Did YAGNI versus interface guidance represent a genuine contradiction or abstraction/context collapse?
> **Abstraction and Context Collapse.**
> Ponytail's directive (*"No unrequested abstractions: no interface with one implementation"*) specifically addresses *speculative internal abstractions*. It does not forbid deliberate testing seams or dependency inversion around real external I/O, databases, or third-party service boundaries. Once abstraction level (Pattern D) is restored, the two principles function as complementary engineering practices: avoid speculative internal wrappers, while isolating external nondeterministic boundaries.

### 4. Did Taste versus Liquid Glass represent universal conflict or visual-grammar contextual variation?
> **Visual-Grammar Contextual Variation (Pattern H).**
> Taste (minimalist, flat, high-density, no gradients or blur) and SwiftUI Liquid Glass (translucent materials, glassEffect, layered depth) are distinct, self-consistent aesthetic vocabularies designed for different application profiles (e.g. data-dense web tools vs native Apple consumer applications). They do not compete as contradictory universal imperatives within the same visual surface.

### 5. Did Ponytail versus formal workflow guidance remain conflicting after lifecycle/risk context was restored?
> **NO.**
> Ponytail's minimal, iterative shortcuts apply to rapid prototyping, exploration, and low-risk changes. GSD's formal phased contracts (`discuss -> plan -> execute -> verify`) apply to multi-phase architectural milestones and production-critical integrations. Once project lifecycle stage and risk context (Pattern I) are applied, both methodologies coexist peacefully.

### 6. Did version-sensitive rules account for meaningful false conflicts?
> **YES (61 edges, 9.1% of false conflicts).**
> Rules separated by framework generations (such as Android Navigation 2 vs Navigation 3, AGP 8 vs AGP 9, Google Play Billing PBL 6 vs PBL 7, Leanback Views vs Compose TV, and Swift 5 `@preconcurrency` vs Swift 6 strict concurrency) were initially tagged as conflicts. Recognizing their generational provenance resolved all tension without requiring external lookups.

### 7. Did accessibility/security relationships retain their correct semantic strength?
> **YES.**
> Zero accessibility or security rules were demoted to stylistic preferences or weakened. All 36 same-source security rules in `android-intent-security` were recognized as complementary components of defense-in-depth, and accessibility standards (e.g., `accessibilityReduceMotion` checks and speakable labels) were preserved as non-negotiable requirements.

### 8. Were any conflict edges simply candidate-pair false positives?
> **YES (130 edges, 19.5% of the audit universe).**
> These edges connected completely unrelated tools (e.g. Android build analyzer report formatting vs Web keyboard focus traps) that were paired exclusively due to polysemous keyword collisions (e.g. "focus", "root", "check", "detail"). They have been reclassified to `INDEPENDENT`.

### 9. Does the effective graph contain contradictions with existing equivalence clusters?
> **NO.**
> The graph consistency audit evaluated all 1,009 relationships and reported **0 contradictions** and **0 consistency errors**. The 15 exact duplicate clusters from SA2 remain fully preserved, coherent, and free of internal conflicts.

### 10. Is the reconciled graph sufficiently precise for SA3 to begin canonical synthesis?
> **YES.**
> By eliminating 667 spurious conflict edges and replacing them with precise contextual (`CONTEXT_VARIANT`: 372), synergistic (`COMPLEMENTARY`: 366), generational (`VERSION_VARIANT`: 68), semantic (`SEMANTIC_EQUIVALENT`: 20), and independent (`INDEPENDENT`: 130) edges, the relationship graph now reflects true semantic reality. It provides an unpolluted, high-precision foundation for future canonical synthesis once human approval is granted.
