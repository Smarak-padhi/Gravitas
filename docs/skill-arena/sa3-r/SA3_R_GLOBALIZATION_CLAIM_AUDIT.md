# SA3-R Globalization Claim & Epistemic Calibration Audit

## 1. Audit Objective
SA3 narrative documentation asserted that the 4 surviving global candidate rules (`CANON-GLOB-001` through `004`) are "genuinely universal engineering principles" and "universal truths."

SA3-R audits whether the evidence gathered in `globalization-falsification.jsonl` justifies claiming universal truth, or whether the epistemic claim must be calibrated to match the actual testing methodology.

---

## 2. Examination of Falsification Methodology
In `globalization-falsification.jsonl`, 9 candidate assertions were subjected to a battery of predefined stress test cases:
1. Greenfield projects vs legacy codebases.
2. Systems programming and performance critical constraints.
3. Cross-platform multi-target design parity.
4. Unit testing and enterprise dependency injection boundaries.

### Outcome Breakdown
- **Total Globalization Inputs**: **9**
- **Survived Global**: **4** (`CANON-GLOB-001` through `004`)
- **Demoted Contextual**: **2** (`GLOB-PROP-005` $\to$ `CANON-CTX-001`, `GLOB-PROP-006` $\to$ standalone)
- **Demoted to Design Profile**: **1** (`GLOB-PROP-007` $\to$ `PROFILE-DESIGN-001`)
- **Demoted to Platform Profile**: **1** (`GLOB-PROP-008` $\to$ `PROFILE-PLAT-002`)
- **Demoted to Workflow Profile**: **1** (`GLOB-PROP-009` $\to$ `PROFILE-WORK-001`)
- **Sum of Outcomes**: $4 + 2 + 1 + 1 + 1 = \mathbf{9}$

---

## 3. Epistemic Calibration: Non-Falsification vs Universal Truth
In formal scientific methodology (Popperian falsification), passing a finite battery of counter-example tests does NOT prove an assertion is universally true for all possible software systems, past and future. It proves only that:
$$\text{No counter-example was identified within the defined test set.}$$

### Calibration Required
- **Overclaimed Phrase in SA3**: *"Genuinely universal engineering candidates"* or *"Universal truth"*.
- **Calibrated Authoritative Terminology**: **`SURVIVED_DEFINED_FALSIFICATION_SET`**.

### Practical Impact
Candidates `CANON-GLOB-001` through `004` remain qualified for global candidate status because they survived all 4 rigorous stress tests without failure, whereas the 5 demoted candidates failed immediately. However, the system must not represent these heuristics as metaphysical or mathematical certainties. They are robust, non-falsified heuristics.
